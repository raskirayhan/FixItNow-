# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the FixItNow project welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while addressing behavior and technical disagreement directly.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Focus on the work, its users, and the evidence available.
- Protect private information and do not publish credentials or personal data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith moderation disputes.
- Unwelcome sexualized language or attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing secrets, tokens, customer records, or exploit details publicly.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional engineering environment.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when conduct violates this policy. Decisions will prioritize participant safety and the health of the project.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact path described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include sensitive technical vulnerability details in a conduct report.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces, issue and pull request discussions, code review, documentation, and project-related communication. It also applies when a person is representing the project in public spaces.
